# Paleta de Colores — CaracasAhorra

Definida en [`lib/config/app_colors.dart`](lib/config/app_colors.dart). Paleta predominantemente **verde**, acorde a la temática de ahorro/grocery, con `#77A14B` como color de marca principal.

## Verdes principales

| Color | Constante | Hex | Uso |
|-------|-----------|-----|-----|
| 🟩 | `primary` | `#77A14B` | Verde principal |
| 🟢 | `primaryLight` | `#BADD71` | Verde claro |
| 🌲 | `primaryDark` | `#316746` | Verde oscuro |

## Verdes secundarios

| Color | Constante | Hex | Uso |

| 🫒 | `secondaryDark` | `#818A49` | Verde oliva |

## Gradientes

- **`primaryGradient`** — diagonal (top-left → bottom-right): `primaryLight → primary → primaryDark`
- **`secondaryGradient`** — vertical (top → bottom): `secondaryLight → secondary`
